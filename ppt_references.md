### 🔴 Card 1 — The Problem: Biopiracy of Indian Knowledge
**Category:** Problem Research

**Headline:** Biopiracy Threat to Ayurvedic Knowledge

**Description (2–3 lines):**
India lost landmark cases on Turmeric, Neem & Basmati Rice to foreign patent offices that lacked access to Sanskrit prior art. Over 2,000+ wrongful patents were filed on Indian traditional knowledge before 2001.

**Source:** WIPO — Protection of Traditional Knowledge: Global Trends
**URL:** https://www.wipo.int/tk/en/tk/

**Icon to use:** 🌿 or a leaf/DNA graphic

---

### 🟠 Card 2 — Existing Solution & Gap: TKDL
**Category:** Existing Solution

**Headline:** TKDL — India's Defensive Shield (But Only Defensive)

**Description (2–3 lines):**
TKDL (2001) by CSIR + Ministry of AYUSH documents 900+ formulations across 5 languages. It prevents foreign biopiracy — but gives NO guidance to domestic Ayurveda innovators on how to PROACTIVELY file new patents.

**Source:** CSIR — Traditional Knowledge Digital Library Official Portal
**URL:** https://www.tkdl.res.in

**Icon to use:** 🛡️ or database graphic

---

### 🟡 Card 3 — Scale of Problem: IPO Patent Filing Stats
**Category:** Market Research / Statistics

**Headline:** 40% Rise in Ayurveda Patents — Yet 80% Never Filed

**Description (2–3 lines):**
Indian Patent Office recorded 11,244 Ayurveda-related filings in 3 years (2023–2026), with only 1,261 granted. Small innovators cite NBA compliance burden, Section 3(p) ambiguity & ₹20 Cr+ clinical trial costs as top barriers.

**Source:** Ministry of AYUSH / Indian Patent Office Annual Report 2023–24
**URL:** https://ipindia.gov.in/annual-reports.htm

**Icon to use:** 📊 chart graphic

---

### 🟢 Card 4 — Legal Complexity: Biological Diversity Act Compliance
**Category:** Regulatory Research

**Headline:** NBA Prior Approval — A Maze for Small Innovators

**Description (2–3 lines):**
Under BDA 2002, any use of Indian biological resources for IP filing requires NBA approval — but 90-day timelines clash with PCT patent deadlines. The 2023 Amendment partially fixed this, yet ABS compliance remains complex for SMEs.

**Source:** WIPO — India's Implementation of the Nagoya Protocol & BDA
**URL:** https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/21491

**Icon to use:** ⚖️ or legal scales graphic

---

### 🔵 Card 5 — Technology Gap: InPASS & Existing Tools
**Category:** Existing Tool Limitation

**Headline:** InPASS — Only 1,000 Results, No Ayurvedic Context

**Description (2–3 lines):**
India's official patent search (InPASS/CGPDTM) caps results at 1,000 records, has no Ayurvedic synonym understanding, and requires manual cross-referencing with TKDL, NBA portals & state SBBs — a 5-portal process for one query.

**Source:** BananaIP — Comprehensive Review of InPASS System
**URL:** https://bananaip.com/patent-news-center/inpass-a-guide-to-indias-patent-search-system/

**Icon to use:** 🔍 or search/magnifier graphic

---

### 🟣 Card 6 — Our Tech Foundation: RAG in Legal AI (Research Paper)
**Category:** AI/ML Reference

**Headline:** LegalBench-RAG — Why RAG is the Standard for Legal AI

**Description (2–3 lines):**
arXiv 2024 research establishes RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) as the gold standard for legal AI — emphasizing precise retrieval of minimal, relevant text to avoid hallucination in high-stakes legal domains. IP-SHAKTI implements this exact architecture.

**Source:** arXiv — "LegalBench-RAG: A Benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation in Legal Research" (2024)
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10343

**Icon to use:** 🤖 or neural network graphic
